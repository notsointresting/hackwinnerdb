## What the dataset contains

Facts (project names, hackathon names, dates, awards), URLs, and short original summaries written by
contributors. Contributors are asked never to copy copyrightable text from external sources; if you
find such content in the dataset, open an issue and it will be rewritten or removed.

under its own terms. HackWinnerDB links to those sources rather than reproducing them.
